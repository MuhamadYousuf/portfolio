Design principles

Four rules I come back to on every brief, from a business card to a festival campaign.

P—01

One idea, set loud.

Every strong piece of design says one thing clearly. I find that one thing first, then make everything else serve it.

P—02

Build the grid, then break it.

Structure makes work feel considered; one deliberate break makes it feel alive. The break only works because the grid is there.

P—03

Type is the image.

Letterforms carry tone before anyone reads a word. I treat type as illustration — scaled, cropped and spaced with intent.

P—04

Contrast over decoration.

Scale, weight and colour contrast do more than ornament ever will. If it isn’t helping the message, it goes.
